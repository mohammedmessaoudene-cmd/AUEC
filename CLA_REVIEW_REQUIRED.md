# Contributor agreement review required

No contributor license agreement (CLA) is currently offered, accepted, or
represented as legally sufficient for commercial relicensing.

Before accepting a material contribution to the AGPL-covered runtime or
gateway, the project maintainer must perform a human legal and rights review.
Any future CLA must clearly identify the covered contribution, the rights
granted by its actual copyright holder, applicable third-party material, and
whether commercial relicensing is authorized. A Developer Certificate of
Origin alone is not treated here as a grant of proprietary relicensing rights.

Until such a process is approved and published, contributed files must not be
added to the set described as eligible for an alternative commercial license.
See `CONTRIBUTOR_RIGHTS_POLICY.md` and `COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`.
