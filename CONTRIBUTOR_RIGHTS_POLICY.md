# Contributor rights policy

Issues, reviews and documentation suggestions are welcome after publication.
No material runtime contribution may enter a commercially relicensable set
unless sufficient relicensing rights are granted under a human-approved
agreement.

A DCO records a contributor's right to submit; it is not represented here as
automatically granting proprietary relicensing rights. Until a reviewed
agreement exists, the recommended mode is **maintainer-authored runtime only**.

Contributors retain credit. No agreement should assign unrelated inventions.
Contributions accepted only under AGPL terms must remain outside any proposed
commercially relicensable subset unless additional rights are later granted.
