\subsection{Interpretation}
The evidence supports four separated conclusions. Historical official-runner records support compatibility for their exact MCP profiles. Four selected transport mechanisms causally affect their direct probes. The three AUEC semantic guards are sensitive to exact single mutations, and the declared finite models contain no counterexample. None of these results changes the unresolved A2A and external-validation gates. Claims outside that boundary remain excluded by \Cref{sec:scope}.
