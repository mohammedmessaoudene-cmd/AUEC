# Conformance mark policy

Open licenses do not grant a right to claim certification or endorsement.

Forks and independent implementations may make truthful, evidence-backed
compatibility statements such as "implements AUEC 0.1 draft profile U0" and
must identify the exact tests and version used.

The following designations are reserved for a future separately governed
program:

- AIEW Compatible;
- AIEW Certified;
- official AIEW/AUEC logo or certification badge;
- approved by Mohammed Messaoudene.

No certification service currently exists. `TRADEMARK_COUNSEL_REQUIRED`:
search AIEW/AUEC availability, choose relevant Nice classes, examine INAPI and
WIPO filing strategy, and define fair conformance rules before registration or
badge use is claimed.
