## Boundaries

Public timestamps, Git metadata, hashes, DOI metadata, and contribution URLs
support chronology and reproducibility checks for the identified material.
They do not independently establish complete legal title, inventorship,
novelty, patentability, institutional endorsement, adopted-standard status, or
independent validation of the whole project. Private preparation history,
personal data, credentials, and unpublished reviewer material are outside this
public provenance statement.
